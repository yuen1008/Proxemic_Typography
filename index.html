About the project

A page that
moves toward you.

Proxemic Typography is a reading interface for the moments when attention pulls a body too close to a screen. It makes text more available without asking the reader to perform wellness.

A small refusal

Scroll slowly.

The web is very good at asking us to come closer: another tab, smaller type, denser feeds. This project gives the reader a little more room to return to their chair.

What would it mean for an interface to make room, rather than ask for more?

When a reader chooses a comfortable position, the interface stores only the size of their face inside the current browser tab. If that face becomes larger in the camera frame, the text grows, lines loosen, and the paper warms. It is a gentle proxy—not a diagnosis and not a precise measurement.

03 / EMBODIMENT

Reading happens through breath, eyes, shoulders, chairs, and rooms—not only the mind.

This tool does not diagnose a “healthy” distance. It asks each reader to set a personal comfort range: supported-backrest, upright reading, and closest readable. That design is informed by ergonomics guidance to read with torso support and by studies showing that viewing distance, character size, and individual visual comfort interact.
